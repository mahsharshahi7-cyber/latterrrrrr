Dil Ki Baat

🌸 Letter, Nigar ki di hui 🌸

Jo man me aa raha hai hum likh rahe hain. Kahin galti hoga toh samajh lena aur writing kharab hai toh samajhne ka koshish karna.
Dekho! Abhi jo bhi chal raha hai, woh abhi ke liye theek-thaak hai. Lekin hum hamesha saath ek dusre ke saamne nahi rahenge. Kuch mahino ke baad tum kahin aur rahoge aur hum kahin aur. Baat zyada ghumate nahi hain, seedhe mudde pe aate hain.
Humko pata hai ki tum bhi humse pyaar karte ho, yeh baat sahi hai isme koi shak nahi hai aur yeh baat humse tum chupa nahi sakte. Par tum humse baat kyun nahi karna chahte ho yeh humko nahi pata. Par agar koi kaaran / problem hai toh humse baat karne me bata sakte ho. Mere khayal se har problem ka solution hota hai.
Ya agar tumko career me focus karna hai, distract nahi hona hai, humse baat karke toh yeh bhi bata do. Kam se kam tasalli toh hoga, hum intezaar kar lenge. Lekin jo bhi baat hai tumko bolna padega apne se humko.
Humse baat karne se tumhara career par effect nahi padne denge. Par agar yeh baat hai toh bhi hum agree karte hain. Agar mere me kuch kami hai ya tum jo chahte ho humse woh humme nahi hai toh bata do, hum usme sudhaar karenge. Kami toh har insaan me hoti hai, kuch mere me bhi hai. Agar woh kami mere saamne laoge toh hum usme sudhaar karenge.
Haan humko pata hai tumlog ka status bada hai par humko khud par aur mere papa par itna bharosa hai ki waqt aane tak InshaAllah humari bhi status badi hogi. Aur rahi baat ghar ki toh tumhara ghar bada hai par agle saal tak humari bhi ghar ban jayegi aur achha hi banega. Toh agar status ya paise ki baat hai toh waqt aane pe hum bhi tumlog ke jaise bade status wale ho jayenge. Agar aisa nahi hua toh tum bologe toh tumhara peecha karna chhod denge.
Toh agar career, mere andar kami ya status ke wajah se tum humse baat nahi karna chahte ho toh befikar ho ke bolo humko. Har kuch ka solution hum milke nikal lenge. Par humse bolo!
Haan hum maante hain, hum sundar nahi hain aur thodi kaali bhi hain. Par tension mat lo! Meri ammi bolti hai ki 10th ke baad mera rang saaf ho jayega aur hum achha/sundar dikhne lagenge. Aur hum skincare karna start kar diye hain toh mere sundar kam hone ka bhi tension mat lena. Waqt aane pe hum bhi tumhare jaise sundar ho jayenge.
Tumko pata hai, meri ammi bhi chhoti me mere jaise thi, par badi hote-hote bahut sundar ho gayi. Isi tarah hum bhi bade hote-hote sundar ho jayenge!
Toh agar kisi bhi prakar ka kaaran hai toh humko batao, hum uska solution bhi nikal lenge. Par tum humse baat karo!
Aur agar family ka dar hai toh hum zyada baat nahi karenge aur sambhal ke rahenge. Aur waqt aane par hum itne kaabil aur samajhdar ho jayenge ki humari family aasaani se maan legi. Aur koi gussa bhi nahi karega. Aur humara Allah hai na, jab hume ek dusre se pyaar karwaye hain toh mila bhi denge.
Humko jo bolna tha bol liye. Ab tumhari marzi, itna kuch ke baad bhi bhaav khana hai toh khao, humko kya!
Phir se bol rahe hain, writing kharab hai samajh lena aur mazaak mat banana. Insaan itna hum bol nahi sakte isliye likh diye. Apne jaisa mat sochna ki bhaav khate hain.

Aur yeh Letter wala baat sirf inhi logon ko pata hai:

Farani appi Rumjhum Afsara Mohammad

Toh mat bolna ki yeh bhi sabko bata di.

Aur iska jawab aaj hi chhutti ke baad chahiye. Hum aur Afsara hi rahenge, tum aur Mohammad hi rehna, Mujhtaba ko mat lana.
Aur agar iska jawab aaj nahi mila toh soch lena humse bura koi nahi hoga.
🌹 Yaad rahe jawab aaj hi chahiye! 🌹
